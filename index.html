I help people see what matters clearly enough to move.

That work takes different forms. I coach people who can feel that a chapter of life has to change, advise founders and executives carrying ambitious goals, and help lead organizations pursuing difficult missions.

The thread through the work

I keep returning to the same questions.

Over the years I have been a founder, operator, strategist, investment analyst, advisor, facilitator, and organizational leader. The settings have ranged from retail and startups to peacebuilding, spiritual work, and research at the edges of what we know.

  • What is actually happening here?
  • What matters enough to act on?
  • What are people not yet able to see or say?
  • What would help the next step become possible?

These questions belong as much to a person at a threshold as they do to a founder leading a company. They are the thread connecting my work.

My practices

Different doorways for different kinds of change.

Emerging Clarity begins with the life a person is actually living. Clearly Audacious begins with the leader and the work they are responsible for carrying. Each has its own language, rhythm, and place to go deeper.

A little more personally

The work has changed me as much as I have shaped it.

I grew up in Costa Rica and eventually made a life in Colorado. My path has never felt like one clean progression.

I have left work that was still rewarding me, started ventures that taught me more than they returned, found myself in rooms where capable people could no longer understand one another, and crossed thresholds that required more honesty than I knew how to offer at first.

Those experiences deepened my interest in inner authority, responsibility, relationship, communication, and the way a real insight reorganizes ordinary life. They also keep me humble. I am still learning how to listen well, speak more truthfully, carry responsibility without disappearing inside it, and participate fully in the life in front of me.
