I help people make clear decisions when something important is at stake.

The thread through the work

The questions that have shaped my work.

My work has taken me into companies, organizations, communities, and one-to-one conversations. In each setting, I have seen that the problem people first name is often not the only problem they are facing. A strategy can look clear until no one is willing to name a conflict. A successful role can become difficult to leave. A relationship can reach a point where avoiding a conversation does more harm than having it.

I know these moments personally. I have had to leave work that was still rewarding me, begin again without a full plan, and learn that a direction only becomes real when it changes how I speak, choose, and carry responsibility for the people around me.

  • What is actually happening here?
  • What needs to be said?
  • What decision or action cannot wait until everything is certain?

These questions are part of how I work with individuals, leaders, and communities. They keep me focused on understanding the situation clearly, naming what matters, and helping people move forward with greater honesty and responsibility.

My practices

My work takes two distinct forms.

I work independently through two practices. Emerging Clarity is life threshold coaching. Clearly Audacious is Strategic Clarity Consulting for founders, entrepreneurs, and executives. Each has its own language, rhythm, and place to go deeper.

Emerging Clarity
Life threshold coaching

When the path itself has become the question.

One-on-one coaching for people who know something in their lives has to change but cannot yet see the way forward. We make room to understand what is stirring, discern a direction that is truly yours, and bring it into the conversations, choices, boundaries, and steps that shape a life.

Clearly Audacious
Strategic Clarity Consulting

When something is standing between you and an important goal.

Strategic advising and executive coaching for founders, entrepreneurs, and executives. I become an ongoing strategic partner: learning the business, bringing an independent point of view, and helping address the decisions, relationships, strategy, and execution issues slowing progress.

A little more personally

My path has not been one clean progression.

I grew up in Costa Rica and eventually made a life in Colorado.

I have left work that was still rewarding me, started ventures that taught me more than they returned, found myself in rooms where capable people were using the same words for different realities, and crossed thresholds that required more honesty than I knew how to offer at first.

Those experiences deepened my interest in inner authority, responsibility, relationship, communication, and the way a real insight reorganizes ordinary life. Relational practice and facilitation have made that inquiry less solitary: they keep showing me what becomes possible when people listen beyond their first interpretation and risk being more fully known. I am still learning how to listen well, speak more truthfully, carry responsibility without disappearing inside it, and participate fully in the life in front of me.
