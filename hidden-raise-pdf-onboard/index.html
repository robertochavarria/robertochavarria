Your next steps

Keep the beginning small and real.

There is no need to finish the Playbook in one sitting. Start by creating enough space to see what is already true about your contribution.

  1. 01

    Download and skim the map.

    Open the Playbook and read the opening sections without trying to solve everything at once. Notice what feels familiar, useful, or newly visible.

  2. 02

    Choose one live work situation.

    A performance review, a project, a manager conversation, or a role you want to grow into is enough. Work with the situation that already has some energy.

  3. 03

    Make one observation this week.

    Use the Playbook to name an outcome you influenced, a risk you reduced, or a relationship you strengthened. Specific evidence is more useful than a perfect story.
