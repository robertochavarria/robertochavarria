The reframe

Your work may already be valuable. The missing piece is a way to make it legible.

This is not a playbook for pretending your work is more important than it is.

It is a practical way to connect your work to the outcomes it creates, the risks it reduces, and the trust it builds—then carry that evidence into the conversations that shape what comes next.

No chest-beating. No manufactured “big win.” No promise of a raise. Just a clearer, more credible account of the contribution you are already making.

The 30-day method

Build the evidence before you make the ask.

You will use four practical moves to notice what matters, document it, translate it, and make a grounded next move.

  1. 01

    Read the field

    Identify what your manager, team, and organization are working to protect, improve, or accomplish.

  2. 02

    Find the signal

    Spot outcomes, risks reduced, relationships strengthened, and problems made smaller.

  3. 03

    Translate the value

    Describe your contribution in language leaders can recognize, remember, and use.

  4. 04

    Make your move

    Choose a visibility habit, project, or conversation that strengthens your position.
